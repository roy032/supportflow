# Refunds and returns

## Return window
Items can be returned within 7 days of delivery if they are unused and in the original packaging.
Sale items marked "final sale" cannot be returned.

## Damaged or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged or is not what was ordered, the customer should send a photo of the item
and the parcel within 48 hours of delivery. We then offer a free replacement or a full refund,
whichever the customer prefers. The customer does not pay for return delivery in this case.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method: bKash and Nagad within 3 working days, cards within
7 to 10 working days. Cash on delivery orders are refunded by bKash. Refunds are issued after the
returned item reaches our warehouse.

## Cancelling an order
Orders can be cancelled free of charge until they are handed to the courier, normally within
24 hours of ordering. After that the customer can refuse the parcel or return it within 7 days.
