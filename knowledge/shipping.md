# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery times
Inside Dhaka: 1 to 2 working days. Outside Dhaka: 3 to 5 working days. Orders placed after 5 pm or
on Fridays are dispatched the next working day.

## Delivery charges
Inside Dhaka: 60 taka. Outside Dhaka: 120 taka. Free delivery on orders above 2,000 taka.

## Tracking
Every order gets a tracking link by SMS once it is handed to the courier. Tracking can take up to
24 hours to update after dispatch.

## Late orders
If an order is more than 2 working days past its expected delivery date, we contact the courier
and reply with an update within one working day. We deliver anywhere in Bangladesh, including
Chittagong and Sylhet.

## Changing the address
The delivery address can be changed until the order is handed to the courier.
